1740QCA Motion Design · Assignment 01 · Trimester 2 2026

Fundamentals of moving image design

A Process Journal documenting the capture, editing, compositing and animation of a lens-based image sequence across six tasks — from database construction through to motion design experiments in After Effects.

The sequence was shot on a Canon 80D on a tripod outside the QCAD building at South Bank: a single figure walking toward the camera across grass, against a hard-edged architectural backdrop of white panels, glass and red cladding. A moving figure was chosen because the incremental change from frame to frame — stride, posture, distance — gives the animation something to work with, while the fixed architecture holds the frame still around it.

Week 06 · Task 06

Reflective Report

[Approximately 300 words — to be written after completing the Assignment 01 Self Evaluation Form.]

Prompts drawn from what the journal above actually shows, to write against rather than copy:

· The database in Task 01 was built before any images existed, and it held for the whole assignment — what did having a fixed structure make possible later, and where did it still get untidy?

· Task 02 produced four markedly different treatments from one capture. Which reading of the subject do you think is strongest, and what does that tell you about how much of an image is decided at capture versus in the Develop Module?

· The composite moved from stacked transparency to cut-out figures. Why did the first approach fail, and what did solving it teach you about layers as design elements?

· Across Tasks 04 and 05 you controlled the same variable — duration — in two different applications. Where were the limits of the Photoshop Timeline, and what did After Effects let you do that it could not?

· Output 06 was the hardest technically. What went wrong with the time remap keyframes, and what do you now understand about non-linear time that you did not in Week 01?
